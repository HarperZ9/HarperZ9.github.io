Any render becomes thread. Send a frame over from the Retro Engine, the Studio, or the Gallery, or drop in your own image, and the Loom turns its tones into threading, tie-up, and treadling in a real weave structure: plain, 2/2 twill, satin, overshot, or per-thread jacquard shading. Watch the cloth build pick by pick with each row played as notes, or play the finished cloth as the score it is. Export a WIF draft that loom software opens, the charts, or the cloth itself, and the piece keeps its trail wherever it travels next.

The WIF export is a standard Weaving Information File: threading, tie-up, treadling, and colors, sized to the structure's shafts. Open it in Fiberworks, WeaveIt, or any WIF-aware tool and the draft on your screen is the draft on the loom.
