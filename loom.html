Send a frame over from the Retro Engine or the Studio, or drop in your own image, and the Loom turns its tones into threading, tie-up, and treadling in a real weave structure: plain, 2/2 twill, satin, or overshot. Watch the cloth build pick by pick, with each row played as notes if you want sound. Export a WIF draft that loom software opens, the draft charts, or the cloth itself, then send the cloth back through the pixel pipeline.

The WIF export is a standard Weaving Information File: threading, tie-up, treadling, and colors, sized to the structure's shafts. Open it in Fiberworks, WeaveIt, or any WIF-aware tool and the draft on your screen is the draft on the loom.
