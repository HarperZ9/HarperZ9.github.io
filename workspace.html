Where your work is kept

These projects stay in this browser on this device, not in a cloud account. Clearing site data can remove them. Download project files for backups or to move your work to another device.

Gallery, Poster, Loom and Shader Room preserve their own editable project formats, including imported images. Sending an image to a different tool carries the rendered image, not its original layers. Font Lab and spatial studies do not yet save directly to this library.
