Profile

My work is about seams: the places where evidence is lost between systems, where a claim picks up more meaning than its observation supports, and where a human decision has to stay explicit. The engineering and the research are the same question asked twice.

Public engineering practice since 2023, on roughly thirteen years of programming and eleven years running tree crews from the ground. The route was not academic and the record says so plainly. What that route produced is an attention to handoffs. A decision can be locally correct and globally wrong because an observation was dropped in transit, a changed condition went unrecorded, or the person with authority received a compressed version of the evidence.

Current lanes are verification infrastructure for AI agents, compilers and typed effects, evaluation provenance, real-time graphics and color, and the philosophy of made minds. The long-range destination is frontier AI, epistemics, and the cognitive science next to them, with robotics as the point where these questions stop being about text.

Research agenda

Witnessed Systems and Bounded Agency

Made systems may be granted operational standing, and their claims and actions should stay bounded by evidence that can be independently re-derived, evaluation independence that is recorded rather than assumed, and explicit human authorization at consequential seams. Five workstreams carry it. The full program, its twelve-month plan, and what it does not claim are in the dossier.

  • Evaluation provenance and independent witness. A machine-readable receipt recording evaluator identity, model and dataset versions, shared dependencies, negative controls, and whether the verifier inherited claims from the system it assessed.
  • Live-state verification and drift. Separating identity, integrity, behavior, and authorization for an agent or controller that may drift between evaluation and deployment, with MATCH, DRIFT, and UNVERIFIABLE as real outcomes instead of missing evidence laundered into confidence.
  • Proof-carrying agent actions. A minimal envelope for a consequential action, tested on whether it survives tool adapters and multi-agent handoffs without changing meaning.
  • Epistemic and cognitive dynamics. The gap between what an agent represents, says, and does, separating uncertainty from confabulation, memory failure, and policy conflict. Human parallels stay analogies and never claims of equivalence.
  • Embodied and robotic authorization. What evidence a robot needs before crossing a safety boundary, how sensor uncertainty should propagate into action permissions, and how a witness stays advisory instead of quietly becoming a second controller.

The 2026 verification engine

Flywheel

Accountability engine and native client. Python stdlib-only engine, Flutter client. On the public default branch as of 2026-08-03.

  • A verified-inference loop where a model acts only through a default-deny capability gate it cannot talk past. Writes, exec, and external tools stay off until a human grants them, and the model cannot self-authorize.
  • Every tool invocation emits a sealed record binding the capability class, the admission decision, and witnessed argument and output digests, never raw content. Records are hash-chained, signed, and offline-verifiable, and verification recomputes every seal from the record alone.
  • A transitive witness graph degrades exactly the dependents of a drifted action, alongside an append-only learning loop turning witnessed divergences into hash-bound, re-checkable lessons.
  • An infrastructure control package: network egress receipts, credential scanning, isolation acceptance tests, a dual-confirmation kill switch that is off by default with cloud credential revocation still a stub, and cross-layer event correlation that is heuristic today.
  • Capability-typed shell admission with a Unicode-spoof neutralizer, a domain oracle registry across three live domains, a model-neutral router with quota failover, a subscription-auth adapter that consumes an authorized token rather than minting one, and session tooling to browse, resume, and export past verified runs.

The trained local model is the replaceable half of the loop and the engine is the durable half. No capability uplift is claimed for the model. The first preregistered confirmatory run completed 2026-08-04. Across 2,646 certificate bodies through the accept path in nine model contexts there were zero verdict disagreements, so acceptance is provably a function of the certificate. Oracle selection beat random selection at every model size on the solvable task family, scored by an independently written held-out checker with random and placebo controls, paired delta up to +0.32 and exact p to 3.8e-06. The harder family defeated every model on the ladder; that null is kept and labeled uninformative, and the self-scored comparison is refused by design. Run and analysis are anchored in a signed, offline-verifiable transparency ledger.
