The whole body of work.

The Current Story sequence is seventeen works chosen out of this. Showing only the chosen ones makes the choosing invisible, and the choosing is most of the authorship. So here is all of it, and here is how far it goes. Dashed borders mark generated collages and contact sheets rather than single works.

Order the archive.

Each ordering sorts on the measurements above. Headings carry their own counts, so a band is a claim you can count the grid against.

What is here, and what is not.

Each work records the SHA-256 and byte count of the PNG it came from, and the SHA-256 of the JPEG delivered here, so the chain from archive to page can be checked rather than trusted. The source PNGs total 449 MB; these are re-encoded for delivery.

The lightness, chroma and contrast figures are measured on the same JPEGs delivered to this page, and every one of them ships in the manifest, so the orderings and the band counts can be recomputed rather than taken on trust. The band edges are this corpus's own terciles: the split into 55, 55 and 55 fell out of the measurements and was not chosen.

Reference and inspiration images supplied to the session are not present. One generated collage was, and it is excluded here because it reproduces those references, which are other people's artwork. The manifest names it and says why, rather than dropping it quietly.

These are materials, not only pictures.

Open any work and it carries a draw this link into the studio's pen surface, where the whole archive is one of the materials the pen can draw: eight image methods, a generative field blended over or under, stroke-by-stroke replay, and export as plotter-grade SVG or G-code. The picker there bands the corpus by the same measurements this page bands by, and a sheet drawn from a work cites the hash of the file it came from.
