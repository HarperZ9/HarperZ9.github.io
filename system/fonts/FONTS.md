keeps the terms its designer set.

| Files | Font | Designer | Terms |

| `conso-*.woff`, `conso-*.woff2` | Conso | Pavel Larin | The designer's licence. The download this copy came from shipped no licence file, and the font's metadata names no licence |

The author's own typefaces live in `brand/` and `type/preview/`. They fall
under the site's LICENSE, and the preview files say in their metadata that no
retail licence is offered.

On 4 October 2026 the author retired Kilon and Telos Display, the face built
from it, because viewers found them hard to read. The site now sets display
type in Hanken Grotesk. The files stay in the git history only.
