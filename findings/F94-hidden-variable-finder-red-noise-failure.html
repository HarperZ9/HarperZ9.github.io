A hidden-variable finder failed its red-noise control; two fixes cut false finds to 1 table in 60

A tool for finding hidden variables reported one in 5 of 30 red-noise tables and withdrew its first round. After two fixes the rate was 1 of 60 (0.003 to 0.089) with planted variables recovered up to the linear ceiling; the second round is not blind, and its real-data checks repeat known links.

What this means for a reader

A method that finds hidden factors will find them in slowly drifting noise unless it is made able to say "nothing here". Ours could not say that at first, and its own control caught it. Before trusting a factor found in a time series, ask what the method reports on drifting noise with no shared structure, and whether its agreeing methods are independent of each other.

Objection

Round two is not blind. The fixes were chosen after seeing which tables failed, the false-find definition and the recovery rule were redrawn after round one, and the real-data statistics had been seen. Only the synthetic draws are fresh.

What would change the call. Preregister on an unseen open table with held-back labels, and add nonlinear recovery.

From the paper's first stated limitation; our register records no separate objection.

Abstract

We built a tool that looks for hidden variables in a table of measurements and tested it on tables where the answer is known. The first preregistered round failed. On 30 tables of independent red noise the tool reported a hidden variable in 5 (0.167, 95% interval 0.073 to 0.336) against a bar of 0.10, which fired the kill rule and withdrew every real-data result of that round.c1 Two causes were found and fixed: the tool always fitted one variable or more, and two of its methods that share a subspace were allowed to confirm each other. A second round with fresh synthetic draws passed every rule. After the fixes, 1 of 60 red-noise tables (0.017, interval 0.003 to 0.089) and 0 of 90 pure-noise tables (interval 0 to 0.041) gave a hidden variable.c2 Planted variables were recovered in full at a noise share of 0.5, and at a noise share of 0.75 with 1,000 rows the tool recovered 13 of 24 against 7 of 12 for the best linear predictor.c3 On tree-ring chronologies the best surviving variable tracks summer drought at r 0.83 (null 95th percentile 0.40), and on the WESAD body-signal data the best follows the scripted states at eta squared 0.87 (0.23).c4 Round two is not blind: the fixes and the real-data statistics came after round one was seen. A third real table is private and is left out.

Introduction

A latent variableDefinition: latent variable statistics and psychometrics A quantity that was not measured and is inferred because several measured columns of a table vary together as if driven by it. Excludes a measured column, and any claim that the inferred quantity is a cause. full definition is a quantity nobody measured that would explain why several measured columns of a table move together. Tools that extract such variables have a standard weakness: they return an answer for any table. The usual way to count how many variables are present, Horn's parallel analysisDefinition: parallel analysis psychometrics (Horn, 1965) A way to count the hidden variables in a table: keep only those components whose strength exceeds what the same analysis gives on random data of the same size. Excludes a test of what the components mean, and any guarantee when the rows of the table depend on each other. full definition, compares the data's eigenvalues with those of random data built with independent entries. A search summary of the literature notes that when parallel analysis errs it tends to keep too many components, and that its reference data keep no dependence between rows [4, 5]. Rows of a time series do depend on each other. Our searches returned no paper that measures the false-find rate on red noise for a pipeline like this one; that is what one search returned, and it does not show that none exists.

We wanted one tool to stand behind every claim of a hidden variable in our other studies, so the decision was whether to adopt it. The test was fixed before the run: recover planted variables, stay silent on noise, and stay silent on red noiseDefinition: red noise signal processing and time-series analysis Random data in which each value stays close to the one before it, so the series drifts slowly. Here each column is drawn on its own with a lag-one autocorrelation of 0.8, so no structure is shared between columns. Excludes white noise, where successive values are unrelated, and any series with a real shared driver. full definition, which drifts slowly and is the closest thing to a time series with nothing in it.

Preregistration and design

Each round had its own preregistrationDefinition: preregistration research method (metascience) A dated written plan for a study, fixed and hash-locked before any result is seen: the question, the data, the statistic, the controls, the forecasts and the kill conditions. Excludes a plan revised after looking at the outcome, and a protocol written only for the record after the run. full definition, hash-locked before its run on 30 October 2026 (UTC). Round one's lock was written after the tool had been tested on synthetic tables and after a smoke run on scrambled real tables; no statistic of an unscrambled real table had been computed.

The kill conditionDefinition: kill condition research method (our usage, after Popper's falsification) A rule written in the preregistration that, when met, decides against the claim with no room for judgement after the run. Excludes reasons found after the run for setting a result aside. full definition of both rounds: if more than 0.05 of pure-noise tables or more than 0.10 of red-noise tables give a hidden variable, the tool does not control false discovery, and no real-data result of that run is reported as a finding.

Round two was written after round one was seen in full. Its preregistration lists five changes: the two fixes to the tool, a corrected definition of a false find, recovery targets set against what is recoverable in principle, and 60 red-noise tables in place of 30. It states that the round is not blind. The synthetic draws of round two are new; the real tables and their check statistics had been seen.

The study ran three real tables. Two are public and are reported here. The third is a table of measured properties of one person's saved images; it is private, and its two verdicts per round are left out of this paper.

Data and provenance

The synthetic tables are generated by the study's code from a fixed seed.

The tree-ring table comes from the International Tree-Ring Data Bank [1], open data held by the NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information: every North American site with a gap-free standard chronology from 1700 to 1980, which gives 281 years by 698 sites. The drought record is the summer (June to August) Palmer Drought Severity Index by climate division from NOAA's nClimDiv dataset [2], 1895 to 1980, a public United States government product.

The body-signal table comes from WESAD [3]: 15 people recorded with a chest sensor through five scripted states (baseline, amusement, a stress test and two meditations). WESAD is released for scientific non-commercial use with credit. We used 75 rows (15 people by 5 states) of 41 variables, 11 chest features and the self-report items, each centred within person, and we report statistics only.

Methods

The tool

The count of hidden variables comes from parallel analysis, with a phase-randomised reference for tables whose rows are ordered in time, and from held-out probabilistic PCA when a separate test confirms an interior optimum. The count is capped at 12. The table is then decomposed by up to four methods: principal component analysisDefinition: principal component analysis statistics A decomposition of a table into uncorrelated directions ordered by how much of the table's variation each carries. Excludes a method that says whether a direction is real or noise. full definition, independent component analysisDefinition: independent component analysis signal processing A decomposition that rotates the main directions of a table until the resulting components are as statistically independent as possible. Excludes a method working in a different subspace from principal component analysis; it rotates the same one. full definition, non-negative matrix factorisation when no value is negative, and a sparse autoencoderDefinition: sparse autoencoder machine learning A small neural network trained to rebuild its input through a layer in which only a few units are active at once, so each unit tends to stand for one pattern. It can represent patterns a linear method cannot. Excludes a network trained on labels, and any guarantee that a unit matches one real-world quantity. full definition. No table in this study was non-negative, so the third never ran.

A candidate variable is found when shuffling its held-out scores worsens the prediction of columns hidden from it, with the Benjamini-Hochberg correctionDefinition: Benjamini-Hochberg correction statistics An adjustment of a set of p values that holds the expected share of false discoveries among the results called significant at a chosen level. Excludes a guarantee that no result in the set is a false discovery. full definition [6] over all candidates. It is stable when the found variables of another method predict its scores with a cross-validated R2 of 0.5 or more and above a row-shuffle bar. A survivor is found and stable.

Every run on real data carries two controls of its own: planted linear variables at the data's size, noise share and autocorrelation, which must be recovered at a rate of 0.8 or more, and a scramble of each column, which must give no survivor. A run whose controls fail is marked invalid.

The two fixes

In round one the fitted count was forced to be 1 or more, and principal and independent component analysis could confirm each other. Those two methods span the same subspace, so with one variable fitted their agreement is automatic. In round two the tool fits nothing when no count beats its null, and a variable is stable only when a method of another family reaches it.

Synthetic tables

Planted tables have 24 columns built from 3 hidden variables by linear, sparse or nonlinear mixing, with a noise shareDefinition: noise share statistics The fraction of each column's variance that is independent noise and so carries no trace of the planted variables. Excludes measurement error in the labels used for the outside checks. full definition of 0.5, 0.75 or 0.9 and 100, 300 or 1,000 rows, 4 tables per cell and 108 in all. A planted variable counts as recovered when the survivors predict it at a cross-validated R2 of 0.7 or more. Pure-noise tables are Gaussian, 30 at each of the three row counts. Red-noise tables have 250 rows and 16 independent columns, each following

xt=ϕxt−1+et(1)

with ϕ = 0.8 and et white noise.

The oracle shareDefinition: oracle share research method (our usage) The share of planted variables that the best linear predictor from all columns recovers at the same threshold the tool is scored on. No linear method can do better, so it is the ceiling. Excludes the ceiling for nonlinear methods given unlimited data. full definition is the share of planted variables that the best linear predictor from all columns recovers at the same 0.7 threshold, computed on separate large tables. It is the ceiling for any linear method. A survivor on planted data is a false find, in round two, when the planted variables predict its scores at an R2 below 0.25 with both a linear and a nearest-neighbour decoder. Round one used a linear R2 below 0.5.

Checks on real tables

The outside labels never enter the tool. For trees, each survivor's yearly scores are correlated with the drought index weighted by that survivor's own site loadings; the statistic is the largest absolute correlation over survivors, and the null repeats it 999 times with a phase-randomised drought index. For WESAD the statistic is the largest eta squaredDefinition: eta squared statistics The share of a quantity's variance that lies between groups, here between the five scripted states. 0 means the groups do not differ and 1 means the groups explain everything. Excludes a statement about which groups differ or in which direction. full definition of the five states over survivors,

η2=SbetweenStotal(2)

the share of a variable's variance that lies between states, and the null permutes states within person 999 times. With 999 draws the smallest possible p value is 0.001.

Controls and planted checks

The noise and red-noise tables are the controlDefinition: control statistics and experimental design A version of the data with the structure under test removed (for example shuffled or pooled), scored the same way as the real data, so the real score has something to beat. Excludes a comparison with a different dataset or with a published number. full definitions for false finds, and the planted tables are the planted checkDefinition: planted check research method; called a positive control in experimental science Data built so that the right answer is known, run through the same analysis before the real data, to show that the analysis can see the effect when it is there. Excludes any check made on the real data, which cannot show what the analysis misses. full definition for recovery. The rates in the next section carry Wilson 95% intervals.

In round one the control built into the tree-ring run also failed: the phase-scrambled tree table produced one survivor, so that run was invalid. In round two the scrambled tree table produced none, and the planted variables in each real run were recovered: 5 of 5 in the tree table, with R2 from 0.978 to 0.984, and 3 of 3 in the WESAD table, from 0.950 to 0.956.

Before the round two lock, an exploratory check on 30 fresh red-noise tables gave 1 table with a survivor under the new rules and 6 under the round one rules. Those tables were not reused.

Results

Round one failed

On 30 tables of independent red noise the tool reported a hidden variable in 5 (0.167, 95% interval 0.073 to 0.336) against a bar of 0.10, which fired the kill rule and withdrew every real-data result of that round.c1 Pure noise passed, with 2 of 90 tables (0.022). Five of the ten verdicts on public and synthetic data failed: both recovery targets (0.81 against 0.9), the red-noise rate, the false-find proportion on planted data (0.32 against 0.10 under the round one definition) and the validity of the tree-ring run.

Round two

After the fixes, 1 of 60 red-noise tables (0.017, interval 0.003 to 0.089) and 0 of 90 pure-noise tables (interval 0 to 0.041) gave a hidden variable.c2 Figure 1 sets the two rounds side by side. The upper end of the red-noise interval, 0.089, is below the bar of 0.10 by a small margin.

Planted variables were recovered in full at a noise share of 0.5, and at a noise share of 0.75 with 1,000 rows the tool recovered 13 of 24 against 7 of 12 for the best linear predictor.c3 Table 1 gives every linear and sparse cell. Two things in it need saying plainly. Recovery at a noise share of 0.5 was already complete in round one; that verdict passed in round two because its rule was redrawn to cover only that noise level. And at a noise share of 0.9 the ceiling is zero, so the zero recovery there could not have come out otherwise. The oracle share within the run rests on 12 planted variables and is coarse; at 300 rows the sparse cell sits above it.

The fixes cost some recovery in hard conditions. At a noise share of 0.75 and 1,000 rows, round one recovered 0.75 (linear) and 0.67 (sparse) of planted variables, against 0.58 and 0.50 in round two. The tables differ between rounds and each cell holds 12 variables, so this difference is suggestive only. Figure 2 shows both rounds.

The mean false-find proportion on linear and sparse planted tables was 0.043 against a bar of 0.10. This number is not comparable with round one's 0.32, because the definition changed between rounds. Nonlinear mixing is weak: recovery was 0.12 over all nonlinear cells and 0.50 at a noise share of 0.5 with 300 or 1,000 rows, equal to its oracle share, with a false-find proportion of 0.21.

Two public tables

On tree-ring chronologies the best surviving variable tracks summer drought at r 0.83 (null 95th percentile 0.40), and on the WESAD body-signal data the best follows the scripted states at eta squared 0.87 (0.23).c4 Both p values are 0.001, the smallest the 999 draws allow.

In the tree table the cap of 12 binds, so the count is a lower bound. In WESAD the first principal component separates the stress test from the other states (state mean 7.4 against -0.8 to -2.8) and correlates with self-reported arousal at 0.88 and with mean heart rate at 0.85. The autoencoder unit with the highest eta squared is high in the two meditations (3.7 and 4.1) and near zero elsewhere (0.0 to 0.3).

Both links were known before the study: ring width in dry regions follows drought, and the WESAD protocol was designed to separate these states. The check statistics were also seen in round one, where the tree value was the same 0.83 in an invalid run. What round two adds is that the same values now come from runs whose noise and planted controls hold.

Discussion

The failure has a plain mechanism. Red noise has a few large eigenvalues by chance, because slow drifts in independent columns line up over a short record. A pipeline that must fit one variable or more will fit that drift, and two decompositions of the same subspace will then agree about it. Neither part is exotic, and both were in a tool whose individual units had each passed their own tests. The red-noise table was the control that exposed the combination.

Round two shows the fixes work on fresh noise. Whether the tool finds new structure in real data is untested, because its real-data checks repeat known links that had already been seen. By the rule written before round two, the tool was adopted for our own later studies, with the cost visible in Table 1: it now misses weak variables it found before.

Limitations

  • Round two is not blind. The fixes were chosen after seeing which tables failed, the false-find definition and the recovery rule were redrawn after round one, and the real-data statistics had been seen. Only the synthetic draws are fresh.
  • The red-noise rate after the fixes rests on 60 tables of one size and one autocorrelation (0.8). Its interval reaches 0.089 against a bar of 0.10. Other drift strengths, table shapes and coloured noise with shared structure were not tested.
  • The planted variables are mostly linear. Nonlinear recovery is weak (0.12 overall), and its false-find proportion is 0.21.
  • The count cap of 12 binds in the tree table. Non-negative factorisation never ran, so the autoencoder carried every cross-family confirmation.
  • A surviving variable is a stable pattern in a table and no more. The drought and state checks show association with one label each, on links already known.
  • Each synthetic cell holds 4 tables and 12 planted variables, so cell-level shares are coarse, and the oracle share within the run is noisy.
  • WESAD is 15 young adults in a scripted laboratory protocol.
  • A third real table, private to one person, was part of both rounds and is left out here. The correction over the three real-data p values included it; all three were 0.001.
  • Scoring was by our own code and was not blind.

Evidence

Mixed. Round one failed: 5 of 30 red-noise tables gave a false hidden variable (0.167, interval 0.073 to 0.336) against a bar of 0.10, recovery was 0.81 against 0.9, and the tree-ring run was invalid. Round two passed after the tool, the false-find definition and the recovery rule were changed with round one in view; nonlinear recovery is 0.12.
