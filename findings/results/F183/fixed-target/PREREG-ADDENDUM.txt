Same as O4b. Code units.py at sha256 4d84c04e68709f3bf77e3fea7c713681b569b08a7308979fb9bc403b25b7f798, loaded read-only. Seed 20261054 through numpy default_rng, the same draw order, so the shifted grid has the same shifts, rotations and method seeds. d 10, budget 20,000, 15 repeats, seven shifted and rotated functions, ten methods. f_opt is 0 on every function.

New. Each evaluation is counted. A run succeeds when an evaluated value reaches 1e-8 or less; its runtime is the count at that evaluation. A failed run's runtime is its total evaluations. Per function and method: successes out of 15 and aRT (COCO, Hansen et al. 2016, section 4: all evaluations in failed runs plus runtimes of successful runs, divided by the number of successes; infinite with no success). Per function, rank by successes (more is better), then aRT (less is better), equal pairs averaged. Mean over the seven functions. Intervals: Wilson 95% on success rate; 95% percentile bootstrap (2,000 resamples of the 15 paired repeats) on aRT and mean rank.

Clipping. Run CMA-ES three ways: A as in O4b (evaluate the clipped point, update with the unclipped sample plus quadratic penalty); B repair (evaluate the clipped point and update with it, no penalty term active); C evaluate the unclipped point, update as A. Count how many CMA-ES samples fall outside the box.
