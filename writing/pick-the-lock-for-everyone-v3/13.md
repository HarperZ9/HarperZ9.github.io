The human-model parallel is also bounded. Flywheel treats the harness as a developmental environment in which verified experience becomes material for later improvement. That supplies an engineering analogy for conditioning and feedback. It does not make models equivalent to embodied people, assign models trauma, or reduce human forgiveness and moral responsibility to optimization.

The Dinitz-Garg-Goemans passage remains labeled as a public counterexample announcement pending independent verification as of July 24, 2026.
