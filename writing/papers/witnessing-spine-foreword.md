## Foreword, 2 October 2026

The text below is The Witnessing Spine as I deposited it on 20 June 2026 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20778927). It stays as deposited, and the deposit is the version to cite. This foreword is new. The record behind it shows a short path. A verification tool and the philosophy appeared in the same week of June, and they joined into one program between 17 and 19 June, when the claim that proving identity does not grant permission became code. What follows traces that path from dated commits, file times and my own session turns. It adds no claim to the deposit and changes none of its status labels.

The philosophy started as working notes for an ontology thesis. In the first week of June 2026 I opened a Claude Code session, pointed the model at those notes and asked it to steelman them. I framed the project as an ontological nihilism thesis built around the Golem story. The word emet, Hebrew for truth, is written on the clay, and rubbing out one letter leaves met, dead. I said from the start that the work was part thesis in philosophy and part thesis in AI research, and that each part should show its value on its own. The session's working files carry dates from 6 to 8 June. A model wrote most of the argument in that session, and the Conferred Existence pages say which parts are mine. That corpus was deposited on 20 June 2026 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20773724).

The first re-derivation tool is about a day older than those files. EMET, named for the same Hebrew word, has its first public commit on 6 June 2026. That commit describes a verifier in which trust is earned by reproduction and never by authority. EMET checks whether bytes still match their source and answers with a closed verdict: MATCH, DRIFT or UNVERIFIABLE. The deposit below uses that vocabulary throughout. During the thesis session I called emet the earlier exception layer of my tooling. So the record puts the tool and the thesis in the same week. It cannot say which idea came first in my own thinking, because my notes carry no date of first writing.

The program took shape at the membrane. In the thesis session I asked that the work tie back to meaning, to the 1 and the 0, and to the seam between them that acts as a membrane. The finished corpus turned that image into a claim: proving who sent a signal does not prove that the sender permitted the action. On 17 June the claim became code. The proof-surface package gained an authorization receipt, a record of an explicit grant from a named human that expires and can be revoked, with default deny for anything the grant leaves out. The same day, the coherence-membrane package added a read gate for witnessed perception. On 19 June I packaged both into Accountable Surface. Its first commit states that the model cannot supply its own authorization and that a missing operator grant means deny.

The authorization receipt had a predecessor. Before June my security work studied how a model's working context can be shaped from outside the model. The commit that added the receipt calls it the honest inversion of that earlier technique. Authority in the receipt is given in the open, by a person, for a limited time.

The Witnessing Spine came next. On 20 June 2026 its five adversarial steelmans and this synthesis were published and deposited on the same day. It is the first place where the philosophy and the tools were written up as one claim: nothing self-warrants. Its own section 8 labels the strongest step, that the four altitudes are one gap, an abductive bid.

Re-derivation moved to the center of the program over the next weeks. Reconcile followed on 21 June and The Conservation of Faithfulness on 23 June. Flywheel began on 6 July 2026 as a verified-inference harness. An internal analysis of 4 August proposed re-derivable correctness, where a stranger re-runs a result and confirms it, as the main direction for the work. Re-derivation is older than my use of it. Debian's Reproducible Builds effort, in which a third party rebuilds software and compares the result, began with a discussion at DebConf13 in July 2013.

This foreword changes nothing about the status of the deposit. It remains a pre-proof synthesis, nobody has peer reviewed it, and its claims still wait on my own re-derivation from primary sources. A model drafted this foreword on 2 October 2026 from dated records and my session turns, and I approved it paragraph by paragraph.
