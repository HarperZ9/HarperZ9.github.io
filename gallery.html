The sound desk.

The same seed that draws a plate can play a piece. Name a seed and the browser synthesizes a short phrase from it: the key, the scale, the melody, the timbre, and the tempo are all derived from the seed with pure math, no samples and no model. The same seed sounds the same on every visit; press play, or save it as a WAV.

seed to sound, the audio sibling of seed to plate: one DNA behind the field, the solid, the face, and now the phrase.

The editions desk.

Every plate above is an open edition. Its provenance is its named seed: the same seed and instruments reproduce the same plate, in your browser, on any visit. Take what you want today: the PNG beside any plate, the plotter-ready SVG, print files from the Studio, or the spatial edition running live. Computer art magazines were selling reproductions beside the printed work in 1976; this desk keeps the offer beside the work and removes the coupon.

The instruments.

A fixture is a frozen frame: a layer stack, a seed string, and nothing else. These twelve tiles are the contact sheet behind the plates above; keep a seed and its frame is stable down to the pixel, change it and the whole composition moves.
