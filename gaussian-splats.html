Look closely. Then move.

  1. Start at the original camera angle. In the Atlas, turn on the source curtain to check fine edges, faces and color against the artwork.
  2. Move slowly off-axis. Watch for holes, floating fragments, blurred silhouettes and surfaces that separate incorrectly.
  3. Change one control at a time. A more dramatic effect is not necessarily a more faithful interpretation.

The scenes render locally in your browser. The Atlas requires WebGL2. If it is unavailable, try a hybrid world or view the original artwork.
