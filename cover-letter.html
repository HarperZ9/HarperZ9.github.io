Dear reviewer,

Maintainers of 34 open-source projects have merged 41 of my pull requests, counted November 14, 2026. Most follow one pattern: reproduce the reported failure, write the smallest durable fix, and leave a test that someone else can run. Datasette #2815 turned a server error on malformed row URLs into a correct client error, with tests. Hebbian Robotics #157 added a test that reports schema drift in both directions.

That pattern fits technical support, developer operations and QA work, which is why I am applying. I have also done it by phone and chat, resolving Xbox connectivity problems across DNS, NAT, firewalls and routers on a support contract and writing down the steps for the next handoff.

My background is about thirteen years of programming and eleven years in commercial tree care, where I turned site assessments into estimates and job scopes, coordinated crews and customers, and watched rigging and clearances from the ground. I have not yet held a paid software role. The tools I ship, Flywheel and Articulate on PyPI among them, are independent work.

On your team I want to take incoming failures, reproduce them, and turn each one into a clear report and a regression test. If the posting has a hard requirement, ask me about it directly and I will answer with the evidence or with the gap.
