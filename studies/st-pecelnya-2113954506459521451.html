What it is, and who made it

An 84 s close-up of a brush drawing a spiral into thick purple paint; the ridges stay where the brush leaves them.

Parts, layer by layer

  • Paint: thick, glossy purple on white (O).
  • Motion: mean speed 0.63 widths per second, coherence 0.82, the highest in the batch (M): one current.
  • Loop: closure 0.93 (M).
  • Fluid reading: viscosity; additive pigment; advection, layering.

The framework behind it

A yield-stress fluid: paint flows only where the brush pushes it and holds its shape elsewhere, so the path is recorded as ridges.

The same idea in distant fields

  • Toothpaste and mayonnaise (Bingham fluids).
  • Zen gardens raked in sand.
