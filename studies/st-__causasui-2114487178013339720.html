Simulacroom (cont.), causasui

Post by Causa sui (@__causasui), 2026-10-25

What it is, and who made it

A 4.4 s clip that moves one figure through four media: a pixel figure, vertical text, barcode bars and glyphs, then a photograph of a person holding a clear sheet.

Parts, layer by layer

  • Sequence (O): pixel figure → vertical text column → barcode bars with broken glyphs → photograph with a clear acrylic sheet.
  • Motion: mean speed 0.29 widths per second (fast), coherence 0.42, mean |div| 0.136 (M): cuts and wipes rather than a current.
  • Type: monospace capitals, partly blacked out (O).

The framework behind it

A transformation chain: one subject carried through media in order, each medium a filter. The clear sheet at the end is the medium made visible.

The same idea in distant fields

  • Translation chains (a text through several languages).
  • Signal paths in audio (each stage colours the sound).
  • Printmaking states (proof after proof).

Dual edge

Chains can launder an image's source; keep provenance per stage (where verification is the subject).
