kinankin, waves on rocks

Post by kinan (@kinankin), 2026-10-24

What it is, and who made it

A 21 s phone video of grey sea breaking on dark rocks.

Parts, layer by layer

  • Sea: grey swell, white foam lines, rocks in the foreground (O).
  • Motion: mean speed 0.054, coherence 0.51, loop closure 2.99 (not a loop, M).
  • Texture: wavelength 0.0128 of width (foam texture, not the swell), spectral slope 2.34 (M).
  • Sound: the post is about the sound (O); no audio analysed here.
  • Fluid reading: turbulence; additives wind and sound; waves, foam, spray; advection, erosion.

The framework behind it

Breaking waves: wind-driven swell steepens over shallows and breaks into foam; the sound is broadband noise with a slow envelope (inferred).

The same idea in distant fields

  • Pink and brown noise used for sleep.
  • Coastal erosion.
  • Crowd murmur (many small sources, one envelope).

Dual edge

Calming loops can become attention traps; give them an end.
