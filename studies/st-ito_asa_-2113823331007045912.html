ito_asa, light on waves

Post by asa (@ito_asa_), 2026-10-24

What it is, and who made it

Four paintings of sun glitter on dark wavelets, built from stacked horizontal strokes.

Parts, layer by layer

  • Palette: #ab9b7b, #877e64, #706753 (M).
  • Strokes: short horizontal dabs, brighter toward the glitter path (O).
  • Measures: wavelength 0.018 of width (the stroke pitch), spectral slope 2.52, edge softness 12.1 (M).
  • Fluid reading: refractive index, opacity; additive light; glints, reflections, waves; refraction, layering.

The framework behind it

Sun glitter: each wavelet facet that tilts the right way reflects the sun; the glitter path is where the tilt distribution allows it. The painter draws the facets as strokes.

The same idea in distant fields

  • Oceanography: the glitter pattern measures sea-surface slope (the Cox-Munk method, from memory, moderate confidence).
  • Pointillism.
  • Specular highlights on brushed metal.

Dual edge

None beyond credit.
