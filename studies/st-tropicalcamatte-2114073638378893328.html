Rembrandt etchings (exhibition photographs)

Post by Tarbram (@tropicalcamatte), 2026-10-24

What it is, and who made it

Four phone photos of framed Rembrandt etchings, one through a magnifying glass: deep blacks, light falling in shafts.

Parts, layer by layer

  • Medium: ink printed from an etched copper plate (O for etching; plate inferred).
  • Tone: dense crosshatching for darks, open paper for light (O).
  • Measures: spectral slope 2.59, edge softness 20.1 (soft, from photographing behind glass, M).
  • Fluid reading: opacity; additives acid and ink; erosion, sedimentation, layering.

The framework behind it

Etching: acid bites lines through a wax ground into the plate (erosion), ink settles in the bitten lines (sedimentation) and transfers under pressure. Tone comes from line density, as in our intaglio canon.

The same idea in distant fields

  • River canyons cut by flow.
  • Photolithography in chip making.
  • Erosion maps in terrain generation.

Dual edge

None; public-domain prints. Photos are the sharer's.
