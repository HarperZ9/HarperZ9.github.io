Kitada Aya, underwater paintings (Musashino Art University festival 2026)

Post by やきしいたけ (@yaki_ceetake), 2026-10-24

What it is, and who made it

Four views of paintings hung on pegboard: looking up from under water toward the surface, in teal and deep green, with branches or figures crossing the surface.

Parts, layer by layer

  • Palette: #304242, #1b2c2f against the white wall #e5dfd7 (M).
  • Depth: values darken and greens deepen toward the bottom of each canvas (O).
  • Surface: a lighter band where the surface is seen from below (O); refracted shapes above it (I).
  • Measures: wavelength 0.107, spectral slope 2.46, edge softness 7.75 (soft, M).
  • Fluid reading: refractive index, opacity, stratification; additive light; caustics and reflections; refraction, layering.

The framework behind it

The view from inside the medium: light enters through a bright window at the surface (Snell's window, inferred) and is absorbed with depth, red first, so the colour cools and darkens downward.

The same idea in distant fields

  • Underwater photography and diving.
  • Atmospheric perspective (the same absorption, in air).
  • Stained glass seen from inside a nave.

Dual edge

Immersion can overwhelm; a site that floods the view should let the reader surface.
