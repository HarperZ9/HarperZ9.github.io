janjirasun, semi-transparent pages over opaque ones

Post by janjira (@janjirasun), 2026-10-24

What it is, and who made it

Four photos of printed matter where translucent sheets lie over opaque ones: numbered colour bands, yellow vellum over pages, a translucent cover, large overlapping coloured characters.

Parts, layer by layer

  • Sheets: translucent printed layers over opaque print (O).
  • Type: large numerals and characters in overlapping colours (O).
  • Palette: #e2e3e5, #c7c4ca, #969bae (M); spectral slope 1.69 (M).
  • Fluid reading: opacity; additives text and light; layering.

The framework behind it

Layer as context: the translucent page adds meaning to what lies under it without hiding it. The same sheet model as Pink Noise.

The same idea in distant fields

  • Anatomical overlays.
  • Tracing paper in architecture.
  • Subtitles over film.

Dual edge

Layers can bury a source under its gloss; keep the source sheet reachable.
