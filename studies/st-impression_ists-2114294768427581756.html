Impressionism thread, with Van Gogh's The Starry Night

Post by Impressions (@impression_ists), 2026-10-25

What it is, and who made it

A thread on the move from Impressionism to modern art; the images include The Starry Night.

Parts, layer by layer

  • Sky: swirling strokes around stars and moon (O).
  • Palette: #5f799b, #394f7d, #171c1f (M).
  • Measures: spectral slope 1.69 (rough, mark-dense), wavelength 0.005 of width (stroke scale), edge softness 3.6 (M).
  • Fluid reading: turbulence; additives wind and light; advection.

The framework behind it

Painted turbulence: eddies at several sizes drawn as strokes. Published analyses report that the painting's luminance has turbulence-like scaling (from memory, moderate confidence).

The same idea in distant fields

  • Kolmogorov's energy cascade (big eddies feed small ones).
  • Atmospheric eddies in satellite images.
  • Marbled paper.

Dual edge

Van Gogh's sky is so familiar that imitation reads as pastiche; use the cascade, not the look.
