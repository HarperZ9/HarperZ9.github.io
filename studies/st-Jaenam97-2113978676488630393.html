Jaenam97, Prismatic Clouds (GLSL)

Post by Jae (@Jaenam97), 2026-10-24

What it is, and who made it

A 21 s shader: clouds trapped inside a rotating glass dodecahedron with a diffraction-grating surface, in a dark room under strip lights.

Parts, layer by layer

  • Object: a dodecahedron with rainbow-fringed faces (O).
  • Inside: soft white clouds (O).
  • Motion: mean speed 0.091, coherence 0.58, loop closure 7.8 (not a loop, M).
  • Fluid reading: refractive index, opacity; additive light; caustics; refraction.

The framework behind it

Two media in one object: a volume (cloud) inside a dispersive solid (grating glass). Light passes through both.

The same idea in distant fields

  • Snow globes and paperweights.
  • Holographic foil.
  • Ice crystals making halos.

Dual edge

Iridescent objects read as luxury; keep them where the optics mean something.
