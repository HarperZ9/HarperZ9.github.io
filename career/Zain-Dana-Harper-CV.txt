Daylight Commons | Independent, offline-first evidence for readers

Built a browser extension that shows the sourced evidence behind replies on nine platforms: X, YouTube, Reddit, Facebook, Instagram, Bluesky, Threads, TikTok and Mastodon. Each sign names its source, states what it does not prove, and shows its measured precision or "not measured yet"; it never calls an account a bot. No server, account or analytics, no data sold or shared, and saved evidence chains verify offline. Version 3.1.0, released October 23, 2026 under AGPL-3.0-only, ships reproducible packages with signed checksums and a full-source git bundle. No Mozilla-signed build exists yet, and the campaign lens is not yet measured.

raw-native | C++ renderer with a CPU reference and checked GPU backends

raw-native draws each scene on a CPU reference and on the GPU, then writes a certificate stating whether the two agree within committed tolerances. Release 0.8.0, October 14, 2026, renders through D3D12 and WebGPU and passed 60 of 60 tests with MSVC and GCC; no hardware-GPU render was checked for that release.

Merged since 0.8.0 and not yet released: a Vulkan 1.3 backend whose 600 presented frames on lavapipe in CI match the offscreen and D3D12 WARP renders hash for hash; the painterly shader ported to the C++ core, equal to the JavaScript version across fifteen cases; a tilt-shift lens that repaints out-of-focus areas with coarser brushwork and no blur, about 0.86 ms a frame at 1080p on an RTX 4090; and a character asset toolchain that turns brush strokes into signed distance fields, game meshes, UV atlases and baked maps.
