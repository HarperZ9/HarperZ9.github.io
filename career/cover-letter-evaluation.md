I have not yet held a paid software role or a paid red-team engagement. For eleven years I worked in commercial tree care, scoping jobs and watching rigging and clearances from the ground.

On your team I want to build the planted faults and controls that show whether an evaluation can pass a wrong result, and to write up each finding so someone else can rerun it. If the role has a hard requirement, ask me about it directly and I will answer with the evidence or with the gap.
