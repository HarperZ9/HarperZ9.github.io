## ATS and submission practice

The exports use selectable text, one reading column, ordinary headings, and contact details in the document body. They do not use skill charts, photos, text boxes, tables, hidden keywords, or invented qualifications. Use the format requested by each employer and review the fields populated after upload. No commercial ATS score or universal parser guarantee is claimed.
