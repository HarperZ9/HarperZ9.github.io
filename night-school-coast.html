The Lantern Coast. Every lesson is a light on the shore.

Night School's 30 lessons, drawn where they happen: the hill where Pylon rolls a ball downhill, the dock and the lighthouse where language models are built, the boatyard, the cannery, the tide tables and Rerun's prism. A filled light is a lesson you can open now; a hollow one is planned. The faint paths join each lesson to the ones that help first. Nothing is locked.

The map

29 of 30 lights are lit. Point at a light, or move to it with the Tab key, to read its lesson; press it to go there. The same map is written out below.
