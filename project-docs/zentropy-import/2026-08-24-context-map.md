## Existing local syntheses incorporated

The import reconciles four previously produced public-safe syntheses:

- project context catalog
- invisible harness user-experience brief
- incident corpus synthesis
- AI infrastructure safety and governance synthesis

Their durable conclusions are carried forward here rather than copying opaque chat citations:

- Event time, publication time, and observation time are separate fields.
- A source can report its own actions without independently verifying their effectiveness.
- Affected-party, developer, government, evaluator, and independent-review records have different roles.
- Incompatible severity taxonomies must remain namespaced.
- Pending reviews stay pending until their own reports appear.
- Corrections preserve the earlier record.
- A public claim requires a portable URL and a stated evidence boundary.

## Long-term intent supported by the corpus

The recurring objective is a lifelong scholarly practice spanning accountable AI, research methods, psychology, philosophy, education, and public knowledge. The public briefing supports that objective as a repeatable research fixture: it turns fast-moving safety claims into a dated, inspectable record that another reader can challenge or reproduce.

## Exclusions and limitations

- No personal health content is copied, summarized, or used to make public claims.
- No operational chemical, cyber-intrusion, ransomware, social-engineering, or evasion instructions are imported.
- ChatGPT content-reference identifiers are not portable evidence and are not published.
- This map proves only what was visible to the project reader on the observation date. It does not prove that the visible list is a complete export of every historic project artifact.
