The Playground

The Playground is a rendering engine that turns one description into picture, type and sound. It has four parts: the native renderer, Superframe at the core, the instruments and study tools, and Low Water, the game made with it.

It is also a place to make things. Blend materials and watch one blend become colour, lettering, timing and pen lines. Mint a typeface and save the font file. Play one of five pieces of music and save it as a sound file. Write a shader, watch it run, and send its frame to the loom, the plotter and a relief. Build words from turning circles in the Fourier room. More rooms are being moved onto this page from the pages listed under instruments and study tools.

  • The native renderer

    Renders frames in C++ on Direct3D 12, Vulkan and WebGPU, and checks each one against a reference. Released as raw-native.

  • Superframe, the core

    One seeded recipe rendered as picture, line, surface, type and sound.

Type: mint a face

Zain Mint is a typeface drawn by rule. Six numbers set the pen and the proportions, the forge traces the alphabet again in your browser, and your own text is set in the result. Save the face as a font file or the line as an SVG. A weight too heavy to keep a counter open is refused by name, and no file is made.

Shaders: write one, watch it run

A fragment shader is a short program that decides the colour of every pixel, many times a second, on your graphics card. Write or change one here and it runs as you type. A mistake is marked at its line and the last working frame stays up. Start from four small shaders or from the 254 of the preset library. Save the source, the frame as a picture, or the frame as pen lines, plotter moves and a printable relief. The address carries the shader, so a link opens it. The Retro Engine moved in here whole: draw from a plate, a picture of yours, your own drawing or a scope, and put the frame through hardware palettes, dither, a CRT tube, 46 effects and feedback, with sound driving it if you like.

Sound: play a piece, keep the file

Five pieces of jazz over sea, wind and open air, composed and synthesized in your browser while they play. The notes show as they play. Drag across them to change the tempo and how many notes play, move the key, raise or lower each instrument, and blend the first band into the second. A seed sets the order of the sections, the piano's rhythm, the fills and how the lead develops its theme, so one seed always gives the same performance. Save what you hear as a sound file at a measured loudness. Nothing plays until you press.

The field: light, flow, measure and shape in one place

Light crosses a Luneburg glass and a body of stirred water. Neither is a room: refraction, flow, measurement, shape, a voice, a map and a circuit are properties, and every element here can carry every one of them at any weight from 0 to 1. Each element's edge is a gradient, so where the glass meets the water the light bends by both at once. Choose an element, move its sliders, drag it or move it with the arrow keys. The probe reads the water and the CAD rule dimensions it, with a tolerance on every number. Every reading is simulated and says so.
