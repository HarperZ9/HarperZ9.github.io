The Playground

The Playground is a rendering engine that turns one description into picture, type and sound. It has four parts: the native renderer, Superframe at the core, the instruments and study tools, and Low Water, the game made with it.

It is also a place to make things. Blend materials and watch one blend become colour, lettering, timing and pen lines. Mint a typeface and save the font file. Play one of five pieces of music and save it as a sound file. More rooms are being moved onto this page from the pages listed under instruments and study tools.

  • The native renderer

    Renders frames in C++ on Direct3D 12, Vulkan and WebGPU, and checks each one against a reference. Released as raw-native.

  • Superframe, the core

    One seeded recipe rendered as picture, line, surface, type and sound.

  • Low Water

    The game made with The Playground.

One blend, read three ways

Three materials from the element table are blended by share. The blend table works out the mix and hands it on. The lettering sets the blend's name in the technique of its leading part, the motion curve draws the timing the blend moves on, and the pen study inks its pens with the blend's colours. Change a share and all three follow.

Type: mint a face

Zain Mint is a typeface drawn by rule. Six numbers set the pen and the proportions, the forge traces the alphabet again in your browser, and your own text is set in the result. Save the face as a font file or the line as an SVG. A weight too heavy to keep a counter open is refused by name, and no file is made.

Sound: play a piece, keep the file

Five pieces of jazz over sea, wind and open air, composed and synthesized in your browser while they play. A seed sets the order of the sections, the piano's rhythm, the fills and how the lead develops its theme, so one piece has many performances and one seed always gives the same one. Choose a piece, a band and a length, and save the result as a sound file at a measured loudness. Nothing plays until you press Play.
