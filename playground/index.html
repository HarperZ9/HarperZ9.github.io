The Playground

The Playground is a rendering engine that turns one description into picture, type and sound. It has four parts: the native renderer, Superframe at the core, the instruments and study tools, and Low Water, the game made with it.

  • The native renderer

    Renders frames in C++ on Direct3D 12, Vulkan and WebGPU, and checks each one against a reference. Released as raw-native.

  • Superframe, the core

    One seeded recipe rendered as picture, line, surface, type and sound.

  • Low Water

    The game made with The Playground.

One blend, read three ways

Three materials from the element table are blended by share. The blend table works out the mix and hands it on. The lettering sets the blend's name in the technique of its leading part, the motion curve draws the timing the blend moves on, and the pen study inks its pens with the blend's colours. Change a share and all three follow.
