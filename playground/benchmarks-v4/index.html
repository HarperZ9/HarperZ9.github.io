Honest measurement scope

Timings come from a single Windows 11 workstation (Python 3.12.10, Node v25.2.1; 1 warmup + 7 timed runs, reporting p50). Native executables and Zero-C PE binaries incur Windows process-spawn overhead (~4 to 7 ms), which is why short sub-millisecond workloads such as sha256 or buildos_slice20_e2e_triad show speedup in in-process Node V8 WASM (10.98x on sha256, 1.59x on buildos_slice21_net_pkg) while showing below 1.0x in cold-spawned native executables. Long compute workloads (titan_1m 37.73x, indirect_draw_cull 13.75x, kcore_color 6.00x, titan_int4_quant 6.36x, cs014 2.24x, cs005 2.03x) amortize process startup cleanly. Both planted negative controls (corrupted output hash and inverted quantile order) were rejected fail-closed in receipt #68814. This page verifies benchmarks_v4_manifest.json via WebCrypto SHA-256 before enabling filters and does not re-run native binaries in the browser.
