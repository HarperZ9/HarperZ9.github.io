Honest scope and cryptographic boundaries

Before instantiation, client verification checks WebCrypto SHA-256 and asserts WebAssembly.Module.imports(mod).length === 0 fail-closed. The Shamir (3,5) and Pedersen VSS routines use compact research field parameters (p = 2^31 - 1, q = 98784247763) for pure-OSI MIT demonstration and are not production-sized 256-bit audited cryptography.
