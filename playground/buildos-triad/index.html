Honest scope

The SHA-256, AEAD, and X25519 binaries are checked here against independent known answers (WebCrypto digests, the RFC 8439 tag, the RFC 7748 shared secret). The other six binaries are checked against pinned return codes and pinned SHA-256 transcripts recorded when their independent reference oracles passed. This page re-executes the binaries and compares against those pins. It does not re-derive Ed25519 or the boot chain in your browser. The SHA-256 attack is a page-side control: the binary's digest of one input is compared with WebCrypto's digest of the same input with one bit flipped.
