## Other notes

- `evidence.json` `ours-lora` claims match `facts.json`. Its `read` dates (2026-10-16) are one day after this check; the two paper entries still say "to be read by the fact-check".
- Hu adapts the query and value tables separately (Wq, Wv). This model fuses query, key and value into one 32 by 96 table, so a rank-1 strip on it is tighter than rank-1 strips on each of the three. Worth knowing before comparing our "rank 1" with the paper's.
- Learning rates were picked per method on seed 1 from a four-step grid; no pick sits at the grid edge (`lrAtGridEdge` false everywhere).
- Forgetting is the worst case: no early stopping, no old data mixed in. The full fine-tune's look-back score falls steadily while its answer score climbs (0.93 at step 50, 0.26 at 1,500 for seed 1).
- Code checks: `controls.gradCheck` 1.3e-8 against 1.17 for the wrong transpose; `rank0` leaves the task loss at 1.6867.
