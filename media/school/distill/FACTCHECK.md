## Other notes

- `evidence.json` `ours-distill` claims match `facts.json`. Its `read` dates (2026-10-16) are one day after this check; `hinton2015.read` still says "to be read by the fact-check".
- Temperature: Hinton section 3 reports that for a very small distilled net (30 units per layer) "temperatures in the range 2.5 to 4 worked significantly better". Our sweep found no temperature that stands apart except T 8, which was worse at full data (`temperature.full.*.vsT2` all unresolved). The film says nothing that conflicts, but this is a difference from the paper worth one line if there is room.
- The look-back failures in rows 12 and 13 are a property of the one-floor student and 2,000 steps, not of the target. The d16x2 check student did not fail this way (`students.d16x2.full.*.copyAccuracy` all 0.98 or above). Not tested further.
- The best-checkpoint numbers pick the best of seven checks on the held-out rows themselves, so they flatter every arm (stated in `lowData.about`).
- Code checks: `kdHead` matches the paper's loss and gradient; `controls.gradCheck` 5.4e-9 against 0.47 for the broken backward. `pool256` passes = 125, not spoken.
