## Other notes

- Missing limit: the film never says the model is tiny, that the coverage check runs on the generator rather than on a trained model, or that the paired and seed results come from one pair of small students with five seeds each. `evidence.json` carries a does-not-prove line; one spoken sentence would carry it into the film, for example in the close: "All of this is one small model on one kind of text; the method carries over, the numbers do not."
- `evidence.json` `ours-errorbars` claims match `facts.json`. `efron1979` and `miller2024` claims are fair to the papers, with the caveat in row 15: Miller's method for clusters is the clustered standard error, not a cluster bootstrap. Their `read` fields still say they wait for this fact-check.
- Citations: Efron, B. (1979), The Annals of Statistics 7(1), 1 to 26, January 1979, the 1977 Rietz Lecture: correct. Miller, E., arXiv:2411.00640 v1, 1 November 2024, single author, Anthropic: correct.
- The rows-16 coverage run and the widths use different resample counts (1,000 per coverage trial, 2,000 for widths). Harmless; "thousands of times" fits the widths.
