## Other notes

- Typed numbers that should be tokens: "65,504" (row 19; no field holds it, `overflow.threshold` 11.0901 is ln 65520), "64 numbers per head" and "16 numbers per head" (rows 12, 13; `traffic.rows[].d`), "four heads" (row 16; `probe.heads.length`), "three trips" (row 4; three dispatches are stated only in `fused-gpu.mjs` and `facts.lab.measures`). "Four thousand" in the predict question is fine spoken.
- `mib1` prints "64.0", "514.0" and "258.0". Spoken, the ".0" adds nothing; an integer MiB format would read better where the value is whole.
- The traffic block is honest that d 64 saves only 1.99x because of the 16 KB workgroup limit (tiles of 32). The film states the d 64 figure without that reason; the limits segment could add one line: "With 64 numbers per head, only 32 rows fit on the bench, so the keys and values are fetched many times."
- `evidence.json`: `flash2022` and `milakov2018` still say "to be read by the fact-check". Their claims match the papers: Dao Theorem 2 gives Theta(N d + N^2) for standard attention and Theta(N^2 d^2 / M) for FlashAttention; Milakov Algorithm 3 and Theorem 1 match. `ours-fused` claims match `facts.json`.
- `facts.sources` describes the papers accurately, including the loop-order difference and that Algorithm 1 writes m and l to HBM. The film line in row 11 is where that difference was lost.
- Voice: no intent is given to models or chips. "The checker caught it" describes a test result.
