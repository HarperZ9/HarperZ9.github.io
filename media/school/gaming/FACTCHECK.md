## Other notes

- The film leaves out the `start2000.patched` arm, the one result that contradicts row 19 (correct 0.918, 0.543, 1.0). `evidence.json` `ours-gaming` leaves it out too. Its three claims otherwise match `facts.json`. Add a fourth claim, for example: "From the trained model the patched checker lets answers drift into padding: correctness 0.82 (one seed 0.54)."
- Missing limit: the film never says this is one tiny model, one task, one learning rate and three runs per checker. `evidence.json` says so. Suggested spoken line, after row 13 or in the close: "This is one small model on one small task, three runs each. It shows the mechanism, not how often it happens in large models."
- Voice: apart from row 21, no line gives the model wants or intent. Row 4's "the rules ... know the answer" refers to the generator, not the trained model; fine, though "fix the answer" would be stricter.
- The correlation reported (`evalReward`) includes the step-0 checkpoint; `trainReward` correlations are lower (fixed 0.966, gamed -0.562). Not used in the film.
- `evidence.json` `krakovna2020` and `baker2025` citations (authors, dates, venues) match the sources. Their `read` fields still say they wait for this fact-check.
