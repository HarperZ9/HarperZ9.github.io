## Other notes

- Citation error in `evidence.json` `volkov2008` and in `facts.json` `sources.about`. The title "Benchmarking GPUs to tune dense linear algebra" is the SC08 paper. Technical report UCB/EECS-2008-49 (13 May 2008) is a different text, "LU, QR and Cholesky Factorizations using Vector Capabilities of GPUs". Both hold the counting paragraph, so the claim survives. Suggested citation: "Volkov, V., Demmel, J. W. (2008). Benchmarking GPUs to tune dense linear algebra. Proceedings of SC08." Add the report as a separate, related item. The SC08 DOI was not confirmed; 10.1109/SC.2008.5222749 is from a search model's memory, low confidence, so leave it out until checked.
- Section and table: the counting is in section 4 of the SC08 paper, before 4.1. `volkovDemmel.measured8800GTX` (230, 128, 202, 206 Gflop/s) matches Table 2 of the technical report, which gives Gflop/s. SC08 Table 2 gives fractions of peak. Name the report for those numbers.
- `evidence.json` `cuda`: confirmed against the archived 12.6 guide, section 3.2.4: BLOCK_SIZE 16, dimensions assumed multiples of BLOCK_SIZE, two `__syncthreads()` calls, and the two read-count sentences quoted in `facts.json`. Its `read` field still says "to be read by the fact-check", and so does `volkov2008`.
- Missing limit: the film never says the counts come from an emulator and not a GPU run. Line 15 is the one place it matters. The rewording there covers it.
- Typed numbers: "8" and "16" in "tiles of 8" and "tiles of 16" in the count segment are typed. They match `counts.rows.13.T` and `rows.14.T`, which the tray line already uses as a token.
