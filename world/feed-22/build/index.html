Still lines,small appetites

Sand on a vibrating plate collects where the plate stands still. Here each grain is a letter. In silence the grains spell a word; under sound they leave it for the plate's nodal lines, solved from the plate equation itself.

Below the plate, swimmers grow, turn round, sprout streamers and feed them, peck by peck, to the pellets that made them.

02Small appetites

Swimmers feed on a dissolved pool and grow. At a set mass they turn round and grow two streamers. A grown swimmer that meets a pellet pecks it; every peck moves streamer into the pellet until the swimmer is taken in whole. Pellets leak back into the pool, bud new swimmers and divide.

Mass is conserved, so nothing here runs away. Click to drop food. It costs the pool what it gives the pellet: your attention feeds the colony and starves the water.

Written from frame-by-frame study of reference clips; nothing from them is copied or shipped. Plate eigenvalues checked against Leissa, Vibration of Plates (1969), and an independent numpy solve. Data series: US CPI annual inflation from the data-as-art pilot.
