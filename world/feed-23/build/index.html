02The grid

Ledger

Each entry is one prism. Its length is the value, its direction the change: up and right when the series rose, down and right when it fell. Ink comes from the value's rank, side faces print as halftone, and the largest values carry a key ink set a little off register.

The series here is the 49 shot lengths of a brand film measured in this study, on a log scale, so the grid writes out that film's edit rhythm, entry by entry.
