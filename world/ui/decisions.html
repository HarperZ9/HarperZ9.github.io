Your call

The direction pushed on three of the site's rules. The type rule is resolved; the other two are drawn three ways, and the recommended option is the working default in the build.

Nothing here changes the live site. The recommended options are what the shell already does; switching to another option is a small CSS change.

Resolved by the author, 23 OctoberTypography

Two faces only: retired

The author: "Hanken and Conso is not a set in stone rule, we can meander creatively, and all design ideas can be synthesized into unique presentations and novel ideas and material assets." And: "the usage of basic fonts can now be shifted to use our own custom typography".

Each room may synthesise its own type from any of our faces and materials, as long as reading stays legible and accessible. Pages read three tokens (text, display, utility) from one file; the house family from the typeface session replaces the stock faces with one switch. The exhibit room's title is already set in Mint Carved, minted in our own type-forge.

Decision 2Colour

Verdict-only colour

Workspace canon: "verdict-only (verified / drift / unverifiable + ink)... The spectrum lives only in generative art, never in UI." DESIGN-RULES 3: "restrained accents... text or shape as well as color for verdicts."

Keep

Pass   Fail   Unverifiable

Chrome is ink on the ground. The three verdict colours are the only hues outside the art, each with a shape and a word.

Bend Working default

Show me   Card stock, 1930

One material colour per direction, used as a surface and never as meaning: buff card stock in the exhibit, brass in Setback, concrete in Slab. Verdicts keep their hue, shape and word. No text is coloured by it.

Replace

Plain view Show me Rooms

A palette per room, taken from its art. Livelier, and the colours start to read as signals: is the red room a warning? It breaks the verdict colours' meaning.

Smaller callsFlagged, not decided

Smaller calls, working defaults in place

Framed archival plates (default: use them). DESIGN-RULES 5 says inspiration images are never copied and the core style never depends on bitmap backgrounds. The later author decision (V5, 23 October) asks for real archival material, cleared by receipt. The directions use cleared public-domain and CC0 plates as framed objects and lettering material, never as the site's background. DESIGN-RULES 5 needs a line saying so.

The frieze (default: keep). The exhibit's frieze drifts against the scroll. DESIGN-RULES 7 wants motion that explains a change. The drift is small, stops under reduced motion and in plain view, and could go if you read it as ambient.

Running heads (default: keep). DESIGN-RULES 4 bans repeated tiny tracked uppercase labels. The running heads are mixed case, one per room, and name the room; they are a reader's map, not an eyebrow. Say if they read as eyebrows to you.

Provenance on the page (resolved by the author). Hashes, "matches the pinned hash" badges and receipt tables are gone from art, story and exhibit pages; one quiet credit line or a credits link stays. Provenance shows only where verification is the subject (Flywheel, Daylight, case files), and there it is built into the story.
