The September 9 Frontier Safety Briefing records a September 5 revision to a proposed independent-investigation framework for evaluating agent misalignment events. The revision separates survey completeness, characterization trustworthiness, counterfactual limits, and root-cause and remediation limits. It also identifies access a full review would need, including relevant models, transcripts or reproducible environments, staff interviews, training-data analysis, inference budget, and time. This is a methodology update, not a new event finding, an evaluation of control effectiveness, or evidence that reviewers will receive that access.
