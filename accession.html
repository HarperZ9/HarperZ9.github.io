Accession

A family of type drawn in code, released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. On this site it is one material among many: living type draws on it, and Hanken Grotesk and Conso stay as the still faces.

Use it

accession.css declares every face with paths relative to its folder; OFL.txt is the licence, and MANIFEST.json lists every file with its size, coverage and SHA-256.
