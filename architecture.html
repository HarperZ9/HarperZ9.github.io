BuildLang

BLDLNG

A compiled systems language whose function types declare every external capability they touch (files, network, C FFI). The compiler checks laws beside the code, emits deterministic C, HLSL, and GLSL, and hosts its own embedded SQL database (BDB), module universe, editor grammars, and RISC-V/OS experiments.

  • buildc 1.15.0: Algebraic effect rows, checked pure par_for/par_map, offline package lockfiles, and repeatable C/shader codegen.
  • BDB (BuildDB): Pure BuildLang embedded SQL engine with crash-recovery and cross-process rollback tests.
  • Universe & Suite: 267 module files in universe/, five consolidated Build suite libraries in suite/, and editor syntax packages in editors/.
  • RISC-V & OS: emu-rv64 emulator and bare-metal scheduler experiments (os-pfa, os-runq).

The Flywheel & Articulate

THE FLYWHEEL / ARTIQL8

A self-hosted AI workstation, coding harness, and accountability platform for any hosted or local model. Thirty previously separate tools now live in one tree, recording every step as a signed offline receipt, enforcing Three-Plate independent verification, and checking outbound prose with Articulate (ARTIQL8).

  • Core Harness (v2.0.0): Runs models behind one local interface with cryptographic step receipts and offline verification.
  • 30 Consolidated Packages: Canon, Mneme, Forum, Crucible, Gather, EMET, Index, Plexus, Relay, Chorus, Learn, Bulletin, and safety utilities under packages/.
  • Three-Plate Verification: Separates proposer, independent hypothesis mutator plus secret-prefix scanner, and execution ground-truth oracle.
  • Articulate (ARTIQL8): v0.19.1 local prose checker (2,096 passing tests) flagging hedging, filler, and register drift without rewriting author facts.

The Commons

THE CMMNS

A local-first browser extension and cross-domain knowledge archive that inspects public claims in place. It shows where linked sources come from, surfaces coordination patterns and fact-check reviews, and decomposes saved posts into parts (words, media, replies) for longitudinal study.

  • CMMNS Extension (v3.10.0): Inspects sources, coordination cues, and published fact-checks directly in Chrome, Edge, Brave, or Firefox.
  • 43K Cross-Domain Archive: 27,678 unique text records, 7,957 reviewed stills, and 2,907 video motion contact sheets distilled into structural laws.
  • Civic Provenance & Radar: Links domain ownership and published reviews directly to primary sources without opaque scores.
