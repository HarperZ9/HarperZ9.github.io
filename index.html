We are here

A claim is like a creature: it only survives where the conditions let it. Every tool on this site lives in the one white cell. Where do the claims you repeat live?

Check a claim yourself

Flywheel

Catch a wrong answer before your team acts on it. Flywheel runs AI tasks with the model you choose and checks each answer against the record it came from.

Try Flywheel Source

Articulate

Find the filler in your writing, line by line, in the terminal, your editor or code review.

Daylight Commons

See who is behind a reply before you believe it. Rest the pointer on a reply and a window shows where the account is based, signs of coordination and who owns the outlets it links.

Install it Source

BuildLang

Know what a program can touch before it runs. A function declares the effects it uses, and the compiler names the call that reaches further.
